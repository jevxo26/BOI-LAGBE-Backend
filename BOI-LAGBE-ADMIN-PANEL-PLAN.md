- ✅ = Completed

> Full per-entity column listings are in **Appendix A** at the end of this file.

---

## Admin responsibilities (from the PDF)

The PDF defines the admin's control surface as:

> The Admin controls: **Products, Categories, Prices, Areas, Agents, Riders, Orders,
> Payments, Reports, Finance, Digital Content, Customer Support.**

Those 12 responsibilities are implemented across the 16 PDF parts below. The PDF also
defines the surrounding operational roles this panel manages:

- **Agent responsibilities** (per area): Product Inventory, Book Stock, Used Books,
  Customer Orders, Delivery Assignment, Local Warehouse, Daily Sales, Rider Management.
- **Rider responsibilities**: Picks up orders, delivers products, uses OTP verification,
  uploads delivery proof, updates delivery status.

## Status notes

- **All 16 PDF parts are covered.** Every entity listed in the PDF's module structures
  has a matching TypeORM entity registered in this codebase (372 entities total across
  the 16 parts; see Appendix A).
- **`src/admin/academic/`** was not created as a separate module — academic entities
  (Department, Program, Semester, AcademicSession, StudentInstitute, InstituteAgent,
  InstituteDocument) live under `src/admin/areas/entities/` and their CRUD is exposed
  through the areas module (`/admin/areas/...`).
- **Permissions/modules** are intentionally partial (GET/POST permissions, GET-only
  modules) — matching the PDF's permission examples (USER_CREATE, ORDER_UPDATE,
  PAYMENT_APPROVE, AGENT_APPROVE, PDF_UPLOAD, REPORT_VIEW, …). Full CRUD can be added later.
- **Agent store inventory** (Part 6 entities) is registered schema-only; the live
  oversight endpoints operate on central-warehouse stock (restock/transfer/audit).
- **AuditLog** appears in both PDF Part 2 (RBAC) and Part 14 (Finance); the codebase has
  one shared entity under `src/admin/rbac/entities/`.
- **`synchronize: true`** is still enabled in `app.module.ts`; consider switching to
  real TypeORM migrations before the schema grows further.
